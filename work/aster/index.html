ASTER

Digital IdentityTechnology2024

Overview

A digital identity for a fictional research platform mapping relationships between people and systems.

ASTER uses constellations as an organisational principle rather than a decorative motif. Nodes, paths and modular frames create a recognisable interface language that scales from research reports to dashboards and public-facing communication.
