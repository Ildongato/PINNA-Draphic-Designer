COMMON GROUND

Brand IdentityCommunity2025

Overview

A warm identity for a cultural platform connecting local makers, places and shared initiatives.

COMMON GROUND uses circles as both meeting points and independent voices. The resulting system is inclusive without becoming generic, combining a recognisable family of marks with an editorial tone suited to events, publishing and public communication.
